IndusCode periodically offers **free model promotions** that let you try select models at no cost, up to a limited usage quota. It's a simple way to explore IndusCode and experiment with different models before choosing a paid option.

<Note>
Free model promotions are offered on a rotating, limited-time basis. The specific free models available may change over time.
</Note>

## How it works

Free models are available to any user with a IndusCode account.

After you use your free model quota, you can continue coding with IndusCode by switching to either:

- **[IndusCodePass](/getting-started/induscodepass)** — a flat $9.99/month subscription with 2-5x API rate limits on popular open coding models
- **[IndusCode (usage-billing)](/getting-started/induscode-provider)** — pay-as-you-go access with IndusCode credits across 100+ models

Both options let you keep working after your free quota is exhausted.

## Where to find free models

You can find free models under **both** IndusCode providers:

### IndusCode (usage-billing)

In the IndusCode (usage-billing) provider, look for models tagged **FREE** in the model selector.

- **IDE Extension**: Go to IDE extension settings, set **API Provider** to **IndusCode**, and look for free-tagged models.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/free-models/free-models-vscode-induscode-provider.png" alt="Free models listed under the IndusCode provider in VS Code" />
</Frame>

- **CLI**: Go to `/settings`, select **IndusCode** as your provider, and browse available free models.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/free-models/free-models-cli-induscode-provider.png" alt="Free models listed under the IndusCode provider in the IndusCode CLI" />
</Frame>

### IndusCodePass

During promotional periods, free models are also available through the IndusCodePass provider.

- **IDE Extension**: Go to IDE extension settings, set **API Provider** to **IndusCodePass**, and sign in.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/free-models/free-models-vscode-induscodepass-provider.png" alt="Free models listed under the IndusCodePass provider in VS Code" />
</Frame>

- **CLI**: Go to `/settings`, select **IndusCodePass** as your provider.

<Frame>
  <img src="/assets/free-models/free-models-cli-induscodepass-provider.png" alt="Free models listed under the IndusCodePass provider in the IndusCode CLI" />

## Important notes

### API access

Free model usage is **not supported through the IndusCode API**. Free models are only available in the IndusCode IDE Extension and CLI.

### Model improvements

Free model usage may be used to help improve model performance and quality.

## After your free quota

When your free quota runs out, you have several options:

| Option | Best for |
|--------|----------|
| [IndusCodePass](/getting-started/induscodepass) ($9.99/mo) | Frequent users who want reliable access to curated open models with higher rate limits |
| [IndusCode (usage-billing)](/getting-started/induscode-provider) | Flexible, pay-as-you-go access across 100+ models |
| [Bring your own API key](/provider-config/anthropic) | Users who already have API keys from other providers |
